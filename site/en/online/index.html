Online workshop

Run the collage remotely

The online collage is the Fresque des risques de l'IA workshop played remotely, on a board shared live in the browser. One person facilitates, up to eight participants build the collage together, in real time, with nothing to install and no account.

What it's for

  • Bring a group together remotely when meeting around a table isn't possible.
  • No install: everything runs in the browser, on a computer.
  • In real time: everyone watches the collage take shape, card after card.
  • Free and open source, no account, no ads, no tracking.

How it works

  • Someone schedules an online workshop (date, time, number of seats).
  • Participants sign up and get an e-mail with their link: one click on the day to join.
  • On the day, the facilitator makes cards available in a shared pool; everyone places them on the board, connects them, adds notes, and talks over video.

Already got a code or link by e-mail? Open or join the session.

The online collage is a beta. The cards themselves are in French. The service is young: a bug, an idea? Write to us.

Your data

To join the live board, only your first name is used, for the duration of the workshop; sessions are automatically deleted after a few hours of inactivity. Signing up for a scheduled workshop uses your e-mail, kept by Pause IA to manage the workshop and erasable on request. See the legal notice.

Prefer in person?

The printable material is available right now: download the cards and gather a group around a table.

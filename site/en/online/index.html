The online collage is a beta. The cards themselves are in French. The service is young: a bug, an idea? Write to us.

How it works

An experience designed to stay simple, with no account and no install:

  • The facilitator opens a session and shares a short code with the group.
  • Participants join with that code and a first name, nothing more.
  • Cards are dealt, one by one or to each person, then placed on a shared board.
  • You connect the cards and add notes; voice goes through an external room (Discord, Google Meet…).

Your data

No account, no e-mail address: only the first name entered to join a session is used, for the duration of the workshop. Sessions are automatically deleted after a few hours of inactivity. See the legal notice.

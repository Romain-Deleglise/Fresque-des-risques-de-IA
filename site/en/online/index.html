Online workshop

The collage, remotely, on a shared board

An AI Risks Collage workshop played in the browser, together, at the same time. One person facilitates, up to eight participants place the cards and draw the links. Nothing to install, no account to create.

What it changes

The workshop follows exactly the same course as around a table. Distance is what disappears, not the conversation.

Everyone sees everything, at once

A card placed, a link drawn, a note written appears for everyone else within tens of milliseconds. You even see cards slide while someone else is moving them, and their cursor travel across the board.

Nothing to install, nothing to sign up for

A recent browser is enough, on a computer or a tablet. No software, no account, no password: the link you receive by e-mail opens the right session directly, with your first name.

The collage leaves with the group

At the end, the facilitator downloads an image of the finished collage, cards, links and annotations included. Something to share, discuss, or pick up again later.

How it runs

How it works

  1. 1

    Schedule the workshop

    Date, time, number of seats, public or private. You get an e-mail with all your links: the one to share for sign-ups, the one that opens your session on the day, and the one to move or cancel it. No code to write down.

  2. 2

    Participants sign up

    Each person gets their own link by e-mail, plus a reminder the day before and an hour ahead. On the day, one click is enough: no code to retype, no account to create.

  3. 3

    Build the collage together

    The facilitator puts cards into the shared reserve. Everyone places them on the board, links them, annotates the links, adds notes, while the group talks over video.

What about the conversation?

A workshop scheduled with built-in video opens a room on our own Jitsi Meet instance, free software we host ourselves: no account, no big-company service, no tracking, and a unique link deleted after the workshop. The link appears right in the board.

Prefer your own tool (Discord, Google Meet, Teams…)? Just paste its link instead.

What the tool can do

  • Place and link

    Cards dragged from the reserve or placed with one click, one-way or two-way links annotated with a word, free-form notes anywhere on the board.

  • Genuinely together

    Everyone's cursor, cards sliding live as they are moved, text appearing as it is typed. Nobody waits their turn.

  • Finding your way

    Zoom by wheel, pinch or buttons, "Fit all" to reframe everything in one click, full screen, light or dark background. As you pull back, cards give way to their number and their family colour, and links stay clearly visible: a thirty-eight card collage reads at a glance.

  • Undo

    A shortcut (Ctrl+Z) or a button undoes your last action: a card removed by mistake, one link too many.

  • Without a mouse

    The whole board works from the keyboard: arrows to move between cards, Shift + arrows to move one, L to link, Enter to open.

  • Reduced motion

    If your system asks for fewer animations, the board removes them: nothing moves without reason, everything stays usable.

  • The final image

    One button downloads the finished collage as an image, ready to share.

Practical questions

What do I need to take part?

A computer or a tablet, a recent browser (Firefox, Chrome, Edge, Safari) and an ordinary connection. The link you receive by e-mail does the rest. On a phone the screen is really small for a thirty-eight card board: we do not recommend it.

How many people?

One person facilitates and up to eight take part, as around a table. That is the size that leaves everyone time to speak.

How long?

Allow three hours, as in person: about ninety minutes to build the collage, then the discussion. The facilitator's guide sets out the full course.

What if my connection drops?

Open the link again: you get your seat back and the board as it stands. Nothing is lost, the collage lives on the server and not in your browser.

What data?

For the board, only your first name is used, for the duration of the workshop; sessions are deleted automatically after a few hours of inactivity. Signing up for a scheduled workshop uses your e-mail, kept by Pause IA to run the workshop and erasable on request. Details in the legal notice.

What does it cost?

Nothing. The tool is free and open source, with no ads and no tracking. It is run by Pause IA, a non-profit.

A problem, an idea?

The tool is young and we improve it workshop after workshop: write to us, facilitators' feedback is what moves it forward.

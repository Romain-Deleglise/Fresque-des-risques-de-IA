The AI Risks Collage

The AI Risks Collage is a two-hour collaborative workshop, with one facilitator and two to eight participants. Participants lay the cards out on a large board, connect them, annotate them and discuss them, to build an overview of artificial intelligence, from its capabilities to its risks, all the way to the possible solutions.

Taking part in a collage requires no technical background. The cards provide the substance, the participants bring their own perspective.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need any knowledge of AI?

No. The workshop is open to everyone, with no technical background. The cards explain the concepts as you go.

How long does a workshop last?

About two hours, including dealing the cards and discussion. The format can be adjusted to the group.

Is it free?

Yes. The cards and the guide are shared free of charge under the open CC BY-SA 4.0 licence.

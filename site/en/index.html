The AI Risks Collage

The AI Risks Collage is a two-hour collaborative workshop, with one facilitator and two to eight participants. Participants lay the cards out on a large board, connect them, annotate them and discuss them, to build an overview of artificial intelligence, from its capabilities to its risks, all the way to the possible solutions.

Taking part in a collage requires no technical background. Participants are guided by the content of the cards and can bring their own perspective on every issue. Discussion is, of course, encouraged.

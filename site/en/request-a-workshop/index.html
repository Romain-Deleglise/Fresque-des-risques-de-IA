Take part

Experience the Collage, we handle the facilitation

The AI Risks Collage is a two-hour collaborative workshop that brings a small group together around a deck of 38 cards. With no technical prerequisites, you connect the cards to build, together, a big-picture view: how AI works, what it can do, the risks it raises and the responses available. You don't have to facilitate it yourself: join an open session or have one organised for your group.

Why it matters

A different way of looking at AI

A different approach

Neither hype nor doom

We start from the facts and take the time to debate. No magic promises, no panic: everyone forms their own view, based on what we actually know today.

Independent

No commercial interest

The Collage is run by Pause IA, an independent non-profit with no ties to the AI industry. The content isn't sold by anyone and isn't trying to sell you anything.

An enriching perspective

Crossing viewpoints

Curious minds, citizens, engineers: mixing perspectives around the table brings out a sharper, often unexpected understanding of what's at stake.

Concrete

You leave with bearings

Not a lecture but a shared construction. By the end, the collage of cards tells a clear story, your own, that you can easily explain again.

Who it's for

An individual, a small group

Curious, with friends or family? Join an existing workshop, online or near you.

An organisation

Company, school, association, public body: have the Collage facilitated for your teams or audiences, in person or remotely.

How it works

  • A workshop lasts about two hours and gathers from a few people to several tables.
  • In person around a table, or remotely with the online tool and a voice room.
  • No technical background: the cards explain everything as you go.
  • The material is free; scheduling depends on the availability of our volunteer facilitators.

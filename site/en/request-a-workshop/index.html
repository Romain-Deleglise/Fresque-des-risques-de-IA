Take part

Experience the Collage, we handle the facilitation

You don't have to facilitate it yourself to discover the Collage. Tell us what you're looking for: we'll point you to an open workshop, or organise a session for your group.

Who it's for

An individual, a small group

Curious, with friends or family? Join an existing workshop, online or near you.

An organisation

Company, school, association, public body: have the Collage facilitated for your teams or audiences, in person or remotely.

How it works

  • A workshop lasts about two hours and gathers from a few people to several tables.
  • In person around a table, or remotely with the online tool and a voice room.
  • No technical background: the cards explain everything as you go.
  • The material is free; scheduling depends on the availability of our volunteer facilitators.

Make a request

Write to us in a few lines: who you are, how many people, your possible dates, and whether you prefer in person or remote. We'll get back to you.

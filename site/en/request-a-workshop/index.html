How do I attend a workshop?

Attending a workshop couldn't be simpler: just join a scheduled one. Browse the list below, online or in person, and sign up for the one that suits you. Nothing to prepare and no technical background required.

Looking to run a workshop instead? Schedule one from the Facilitate page.

Workshops

Scheduled workshops

Pick a public workshop and sign up: you'll receive a confirmation e-mail with the date and time (and the exact location for in-person workshops) and, for an online workshop, the link that takes you straight to the shared board.

When you sign up, your first name and e-mail are kept by Pause IA to manage the workshop and for any follow-up related to the collage. You can unsubscribe or ask for your data to be erased at any time at contact@pauseia.fr. See the legal notice.

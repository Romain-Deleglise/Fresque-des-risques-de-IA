Attend

How do I attend a workshop?

Attending a workshop couldn't be simpler: just join a scheduled one. Browse the list below, online or in person, and sign up for the one that suits you. Nothing to prepare and no technical background required.

Workshops

Scheduled workshops

Pick a public workshop and sign up: you'll receive a confirmation e-mail with the session code, the date and time (and the exact location for in-person workshops).

When you sign up, your first name and e-mail are kept by Pause IA to manage the workshop and for any follow-up related to the collage. You can unsubscribe or ask for your data to be erased at any time at contact@pauseia.fr. See the legal notice.

Schedule a workshop

Choose the format, date and number of seats. You'll receive a confirmation e-mail with the session code. A public workshop appears above; a private one stays known to you alone (share the code yourself). For an online workshop, on the day you open the live session with this code from the online Collage.

Workshop format

Your first name and e-mail are kept by Pause IA to manage your workshops and for any follow-up. Erasure on request at contact@pauseia.fr (see the legal notice).

Move a workshop

A change of date? Enter the code, the facilitator's e-mail and the new date: registrants are notified automatically.

Cancel a workshop

Made a mistake? Enter the session code and the facilitator's e-mail to remove it from the list. (The link in your e-mail also cancels it in one click.)

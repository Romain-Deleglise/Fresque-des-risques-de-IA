Personal data

The newsletter sign-up form collects your e-mail address for the sole purpose of sending you news about the Collage. This data is handled by Pause IA and is not shared with third parties. Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, as well as the right to unsubscribe at any time, by writing to contact@pauseia.fr.

The online collage requires no account and no e-mail address: only the first name entered to join a session is used, for the duration of the workshop. Sessions are automatically deleted after a few hours of inactivity.

Audience measurement

The site measures its traffic anonymously, with no cookie and no advertising tracker, and never tracks visitors across sites. For each page view, only the page visited, the source (the referring site's domain name) and the language are counted; no IP address and no visitor identifier is retained. The data is hosted by Pause IA and is used solely for aggregate statistics. This measurement honours your browser's "Do Not Track" and "Global Privacy Control" signals: if either is enabled, no data is sent.

Feedback or questions?

Write to us at contact@pauseia.fr. The site's code is public on GitHub.

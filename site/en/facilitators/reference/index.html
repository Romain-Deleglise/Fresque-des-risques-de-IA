The reference fresk

A reference fresk to prepare your workshop, and somewhere to tell us what does not work in the cards.

This page is not public and must not be shared with participants. Seeing the links in advance removes most of what the workshop gives: it is by searching for the relationships that a group makes them its own.

One possible layout, not an answer key

There is no single right fresk. The layout below is the one we use to find our bearings: it follows the five sets from the guide and connects each card to what brings it about. A sound fresk can look very different: other groupings, other arrows, other priorities. Use it to anticipate questions, not to correct tables.

Every arrow carries the cause-and-effect link it expresses. That wording matters more than where the cards sit: in a workshop an arrow without an explanation is worth nothing, and an arrow the group can justify is always acceptable.
